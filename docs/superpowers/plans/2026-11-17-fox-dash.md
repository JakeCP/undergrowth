# The fox's Dash Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Under the trial rule `foxDash`, the fox may, before it has walked or acted, run up to 5 tiles in place of its walk and bite. It is shown, judged and tried in `#lab`.

**Architecture:** A dash is a **move with a flag** (`{kind: 'move', band, path, dash: true}`), not an action. It reuses the whole walk in `applyBandMove` (captures, pickups, growth, freeing), then ends the band's turn. So `actionsFor` and the bots stay untouched. A small engine module, `js/engine/dash.js`, answers "can this band dash" and "where to". The UI, bench and solver all ask that module.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla JS ES modules with JSDoc types, `node --test`, `npx tsc -p jsconfig.json`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-fox-dash-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- With `foxDash` off, nothing changes: every existing test, the bench table and every solution line stay as they are.
- Dash range: the type's `speed` + its `dash` (fox: 3 + 2 = 5). The dash tiles are those reachable in 5 that are **not** reachable in the usual walk.
- Only before the band has walked or acted. After a dash, `moved` and `acted` are both true.
- Walking rules unchanged: no jumping; water, holes and stones block; bands block; `snakeBodies` is followed.
- Copy (verbatim): lab label `The fox can dash: run 5 tiles, but no bite that turn`; card line `Runs up to 5 tiles instead of 3, but cannot bite that turn.`; hover `Dash here: the fox can't bite this turn.`; hint `Dash: tap a tile to run to`.
- Look: the dash tiles get a softer, cooler wash than the move tiles, with no stroke, no dashed outline and no ring.
- Types: `npx tsc -p jsconfig.json`. Tests: `node --test`.
- `sw.js` `APP_VERSION` is raised past every branch's, then `npm run precache-hash`.

## Review Focus

1. A fox that dashes onto a permit, a seed or next to a trapped band: it captures, collects and frees, as a walk does. (Task 1 test.)
2. `snakeBodies` and `foxDash` on together: the dash tiles come from `reachableFollowing` with 5 steps, and the engine accepts that path. (Task 1 test.)
3. Undo after a dash restores the fox to ready, with its dash tiles back. (Task 2: covered by the existing undo stack; test that `selectionView` of the restored state has the dash tiles.)
4. A fox with an enemy beside it and dash tiles too: the bite targets and the dash tiles both show, and a tap on either does its own thing. (Task 2 test.)
5. A dash with the rule off, or by a non-fox, or after walking, is refused by the engine with an error. (Task 1 test.)

### Task 1: Engine: the rule, the dash move, turn state

**Files:**
- Create: `js/engine/dash.js`
- Modify: `js/engine/types.js` (`Rules.foxDash?`, `BandType.dash?`, `Move` move variant `dash?: boolean`, `BattleEvent` move variant `dash?: boolean`)
- Modify: `js/engine/units.js` (fox gets `dash: 2`, commented like `bristle`)
- Modify: `js/engine/battle.js` (`TRIAL_RULE_IDS` adds `'foxDash'`; `applyBandMove` takes the dash flag)
- Modify: `js/ui/turn-state.js` (`ready` also when dash tiles exist)
- Test: `tests/fox-dash.test.mjs`; update `tests/units.test.mjs` only if it pins the fox's keys

**Interfaces:**
- Produces, in `js/engine/dash.js`:
  - `dashOn(state: BattleState): boolean`: `state.rules?.foxDash === true`
  - `canDash(state: BattleState, band: Band): boolean`: rule on, `side === 'us'`, not trapped, has cells, `!moved && !acted`, `(BAND_TYPES[band.type].dash ?? 0) > 0`
  - `dashRange(typeId: string): number`: `speed + (dash ?? 0)`
  - `dashTiles(state: BattleState, bandId: string): Map<string, Pos[]>`: empty unless `canDash`; else `reachable(state, id, dashRange)` minus every key in `reachable(state, id)`
- `applyBandMove(state, bandId, path, dash = false)`. With `dash`: throws unless `canDash`; the length limit is `dashRange` in place of `speed`. Then everything as a walk. The move event carries `dash: true`. At the end, `acted = true` as well as `moved`.
- `advance` passes `move.dash === true`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `tests/fox-dash.test.mjs`, building states with `makeState` from `tests/helpers.mjs` and `rules: {foxDash: true}` in the extra:
  - `dashTiles` on an open 8×8 with the fox at (0,0): every key has Manhattan distance 4 or 5 from the head, and their count equals `reachable(..., 5).size - reachable(...).size`.
  - With the rule off, or for the hedgehog, or after the fox has moved: `dashTiles(...).size === 0`, and `applyMove` with `dash: true` throws.
  - `applyMove({kind:'move', band:'fox', path /* 5 steps */, dash:true})`: the head is at the end, `moved && acted`, the event is `{kind:'move', ..., dash:true}`, and then `{kind:'act', ...bite}` throws.
  - A dash over a permit (`P`) and a seed (`*`) captures and collects. One that ends next to a trapped band frees it.
  - Water and stones between the fox and a tile 5 away make the tile absent from `dashTiles` when only a crossing would reach it.
  - With `snakeBodies` also on and a 3-long fox: every path in `dashTiles` is accepted by `applyMove` with `dash: true`.
  - `bandTurnState` for a fox that has dashed is `'done'`. For a fox that has not moved, with the rule on and dash tiles, it is `'ready'`.
  - The fox's dash moves are refused with a length of 6.

### Task 2: The battle screen: dash tiles, tap, focus button, hover, log, card

**Files:**
- Modify: `js/ui/battle-view.js` (`SelectionView` gains `dashes: Map<string, Pos[]>`; `selectionView`, `tapAction`, `selectionNote`, the hover note, `drawBoard`, the after-move selection)
- Modify: `js/ui/board.js` (`BoardView` gains `dashes?: Pos[]`; `highlightAttrs` gains kind `'dash'`)
- Modify: `js/ui/battle-focus.js` (the Dash entry and its hint)
- Modify: `js/ui/text.js` (a dash's sentence in `describeEvents`)
- Modify: `js/ui/unit-info.js` (`unitCard(typeId, maxLen, rules = {})`, `dashSentence(range)`)
- Modify: `js/ui/deploy-view.js` (passes `level.rules ?? {}` to `unitCard`)
- Test: `tests/fox-dash-view.test.mjs`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `canDash`, `dashTiles`, `dashRange` (Task 1).
- `DASH_ID = 'dash'` exported from `js/ui/battle-view.js`: the `chosen` value meaning "show the dash tiles alone".
- `selectionView(state, bandId, chosen)`:
  - `dashes` is `dashTiles(...)`, or empty unless the view shows moves.
  - With `chosen === DASH_ID` and dash tiles: `actionId` is `DASH_ID`, `moves` and `targets` are empty, and `dashes` is the full map. Otherwise the view is as today, plus `dashes`. The direct/menu mode is decided as today, from the real actions only.
  - `options` gets `{id: 'dash', label: 'Dash', enabled: dashes.size > 0, chosen: actionId === DASH_ID}` appended whenever the rule is on and the band's type has `dash`, even when greyed.
- `tapAction(view, pos)`: a tile in `view.dashes` returns `{kind:'move', band, path, dash: true}`. It is checked after targets and moves.
- After a dash move, the band is let go (`selected = null`), as after an action.
- Focus panel keys: when the options include Dash, every option gets its number key (`1 Bite`, `2 Dash`), in direct mode too. Choosing Dash toggles `chosen` between `DASH_ID` and null. Its hint is `Dash: tap a tile to run to`.
- Hover over a dash tile: the message line shows `Dash here: the fox can't bite this turn.` (fox name from `tierFor`).
- `highlightAttrs(pos, 'dash')`: the cream move wash's shape, a cooler fill (the board's existing cool/water ink if there is one, else a pale blue-grey), `fill-opacity` about 0.22, **no stroke**.
- `describeEvents`: a `move` event with `dash: true` gives `"<Name> dashes"` (the walk itself stays silent).
- `dashSentence(range: number): string` → `` `Runs up to ${range} tiles instead of ${speed}, but cannot bite that turn.` ``. `unitCard` appends `{id: 'dash', label: 'Dash', sentence}` to `actions` when `rules.foxDash === true` and the type has `dash`.

  - With the rule off: `options.map(o => o.id)` is `['bite']` and `dashes.size === 0`.
  - `chosen = DASH_ID`: `moves.size === 0`, `targets.length === 0`, `dashes.size > 0`.
  - `tapAction` on a dash tile returns a move with `dash: true` and the map's path.
  - After `applyMove(... a walk ...)`: `dashes.size === 0`, and the Dash option is greyed (`enabled: false`).
  - Undo: `selectionView` of the state before a dash has the same `dashes` as it did before the dash.

  - `renderBoard` with `dashes: [[4,1]]` draws one `rect[data-highlight="dash"]` with no `stroke` attribute.
  - `describeEvents` on a dash event gives `['Fox dashes']` (or the capitalised form the file uses).
  - `unitCard('fox', 2, {foxDash: true}).actions.map(a => a.id)` is `['bite','dash']`, with the verbatim sentence. `unitCard('fox', 2)` is unchanged.
